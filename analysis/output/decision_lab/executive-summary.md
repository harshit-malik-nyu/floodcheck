# Allocate audit effort without mistaking repetition for coordination

Fund contextual follow-up rather than a duplicate-text enforcement rule. Report what the sampling design can and cannot detect at the available audit budget.

**Observed evidence:** The frozen signal-separation fixtures are synthetic. Duplicate text is not proof of coordination, and the detection ceiling assumes a specific uniform sample and two-member detector.

**Decision owner:** Public-comment audit lead (proposed role, not a client).

**Base planning case:** Metadata triage plus contextual follow-up. This is conditional on the assumptions, not deployment approval.

| Alternative | Economic cost | Cash outlay | Reviewer hours | Passes risk and capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Uniform sample and contextual review | $56,072 | $72 | 800.0 | True |
| Metadata triage plus contextual follow-up | $43,840 | $10,240 | 480.0 | True |
| Duplicate-text flags without context | $7,840 | $2,240 | 80.0 | False |

Economic cost includes valued staff capacity and scenario incident losses. Cash outlay excludes salaried capacity and contingent losses. These are not realized savings.

**Sensitivity across 27 equally enumerated scenarios:** Uniform sample and contextual review: 1, NO FEASIBLE OPTION: 18, Metadata triage plus contextual follow-up: 8. Grid counts are not probabilities.

**Synthetic product experiment:** HOLD. Primary effect 6.8%; 95% interval [2.9%, 10.6%]. Guardrail effect 1.0%; interval [0.4%, 1.8%]. These numbers test the decision logic, not product performance.

**Priority order:**

1. Prespecify the sampling frame and missing-metadata treatment.
2. Separate benign organized participation from evidence of deceptive coordination.
3. Compare contextual review coverage per analyst hour before expanding collection.

**What would reverse the recommendation:** If contextual follow-up cannot keep false accusations below the gate, report insufficient evidence instead of labeling a campaign.

**Remaining uncertainty:** No real-docket performance is established. Harm from false accusation is a hard gate, not monetized as a routine operating expense.
